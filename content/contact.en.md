## What you can write about

- **A mistake on the map or in a guide:** name the country or the page and say what looks wrong. Borders and names follow the official positions of the Republic of Türkiye, as the [Terms of Use](/terms/) explain, but a wrong label or a missing place is always worth reporting.
- **Something that doesn't work:** your phone or computer, your browser, and what you tapped just before the problem help us reproduce it.
- **Ideas and feedback:** a feature you'd like, a place you can't find, or a question you'd like a guide to answer.
- **Privacy requests:** to find out what is stored, or to correct or delete it, see [your rights](/privacy/#rights). For a restore record, include the restore code, because it is the only way to find that record.
- **Press and partnerships:** a short note on who you are and what you have in mind is enough.

Please don't send passwords or health information by email. Send identity details only when a legal request requires them.
