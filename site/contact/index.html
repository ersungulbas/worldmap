Contact

worldmap.tech is a small, independent project, and email is the way to reach it. Every message is read by a person, not a bot.

Email

iletisim@worldmap.tech

We try to reply within a few working days. Requests about your personal data are answered free of charge, within 30 days at the latest.

What you can write about

  • A mistake on the map or in a guide: name the country or the page and say what looks wrong. Borders and names follow the official positions of the Republic of Türkiye, as the Terms of Use explain, but a wrong label or a missing place is always worth reporting.
  • Something that doesn't work: your phone or computer, your browser, and what you tapped just before the problem help us reproduce it.
  • Ideas and feedback: a feature you'd like, a place you can't find, or a question you'd like a guide to answer.
  • Privacy requests: to find out what is stored, or to correct or delete it, see your rights. For a restore record, include the restore code, because it is the only way to find that record.
  • Press and partnerships: a short note on who you are and what you have in mind is enough.

Please don't send passwords or health information by email. Send identity details only when a legal request requires them.

Who runs the site
