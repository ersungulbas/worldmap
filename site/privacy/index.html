Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use worldmap.tech, why, and how. The site is run from Türkiye, so it is primarily governed by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). This page is also our KVKK privacy notice (aydınlatma metni). It includes extra information for visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and US states with privacy laws.

This page is for information only. You do not have to accept it. Consent for advertising cookies is asked for separately, in the cookie banner.

In short

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name or email address.
  • The countries you tap are stored only in your own browser.
  • Share images and QR codes are created on your device and are not sent to us.
  • The restore code is optional. We store only the code, the country list and two timestamps.
  • Visitor statistics use no cookies and build no personal profiles.
  • Outside the US, cookies that personalise ads are used only if you agree.
  • We do not sell your data for money.

International transfers

Cloudflare runs servers in many countries. Connection data and restore records may be processed and stored outside Türkiye, for example in the USA or EU countries. The purpose is to deliver the site and provide the restore feature.

We keep transferred data to a minimum. We do not store IP addresses ourselves, and a restore record contains no name, email or IP address.

If you accept advertising cookies, Google collects data directly from your browser and may process it on servers outside your country, including in the USA. Google is responsible for that processing.

Under Turkish law: as of the date of this policy, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board has not issued an adequacy decision for these countries.

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR: Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, including the UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Framework. Cloudflare's data processing addendum also includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies and browser storage (localStorage) let a site keep small pieces of information on your device. The storage worldmap.tech itself uses is needed for the site to work and never leaves your device. Advertising cookies are handled by region:

  • Türkiye and all countries not listed below: our own banner offers equal "Accept" and "Reject" buttons. Ads load only after you choose "Accept". If you reject, no ads load and the site works the same.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Here we request non-personalised ads only, so ads are not chosen based on your interests. Google's certified consent platform (IAB TCF) asks for your choices about cookies and device access. If you do not consent, Google may show "limited ads" that do not use personal data for personalisation. Your IP address is still used to deliver these ads, and some cookies may be used only to detect invalid traffic and abuse.
  • United States: ads are shown by default. In states with privacy laws, Google's message offers a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link to opt out of personalised advertising.

You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before, but it stops new advertising cookies from being used. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under KVKK (Article 11), everyone may:

  • Find out whether and how your personal data is processed.
  • Learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for that purpose.
  • Know who receives your data, in Türkiye or abroad.
  • Ask for incomplete or wrong data to be corrected, and, where the conditions are met, deleted or destroyed.
  • Ask that recipients be told about such corrections or deletions.
  • Object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis.
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have these rights under the GDPR or UK GDPR:

  • Access, rectification and erasure.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Objecting to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawing consent at any time.
  • Complaining to your local data protection authority. The EEA list is at edpb.europa.eu; in the UK it is the ICO.

How to make a request

  • Email iletisim@worldmap.tech. Under Turkish rules you may also apply in writing, by registered electronic mail (KEP), or with a secure electronic or mobile signature. Ask us by email for a postal address.
  • For Turkish-law requests, include your full name, your Turkish ID number (or, for foreigners, your nationality and passport or ID number), a contact address and what you are asking for. Written requests must be signed.
  • For anything about a restore record, include the restore code. We cannot identify you any other way.
  • We may ask for extra information to confirm your identity, and we use it only to handle your request.
  • We reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Under the GDPR, the deadline is one month, which can be extended where the law allows.

Under KVKK, if you are not satisfied with our reply or receive none, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr). You must do so within 30 days of learning of our reply, and in any case within 60 days of your request.

You can delete your restore record yourself, without any request, from inside the app. You can delete data stored in your browser from your browser settings.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). We limit repeated attempts to guess restore codes, and we keep stored data to a minimum. No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs, we will notify the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board and, where needed, inform you through a notice on the site.

Keep your restore code private. Anyone who has it can view, change or delete your map.
