About worldmap.tech

worldmap.tech is a free web app where you mark the countries you have visited on a world map and see how many you have been to, how much of the world that is, and your traveler level. There is no sign-up and nothing to install. Your map stays in your own browser.

Why it exists

"How many countries have you been to?" is one of the first questions travelers ask each other. The number is easy to remember and hard to show. worldmap.tech answers it in a few taps, with a clean image you can share. Opening a friend's link and seeing both maps on one screen is part of the same idea.

Who makes it

worldmap.tech is built and run independently by Kemal Ersun Gülbaş, who lives in Ankara, Türkiye. The site is free and ads pay for the running costs, and they never appear on your map or in the images you save.

Map and data

The map is drawn with public-domain Natural Earth data in the Natural Earth projection. It has 233 selectable places. Your share of the world is calculated out of 195: the 193 UN member states plus Vatican City and Palestine. Borders and names follow the official positions of the Republic of Türkiye. The map is approximate and is not an official or legal statement on any dispute. Read How many countries are there? and the FAQ for the details.

Privacy

The countries you mark are stored in your browser, and your share link carries the map inside the link itself. Saved images are created on your device. Only if you ask for a restore code is your map sent to the server, where it is stored under that code alone, with no name or email. Visitor statistics are cookieless. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Found a mistake, a missing country, or have a view on a border or a name? Write to iletisim@worldmap.tech. Messages are reviewed within a reasonable time and corrections are made where needed. Operator details are at the end of the Terms of Use.
